# Sounds used in OscreenSaver

| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| glass.wav | "glass shatter.wav", https://freesound.org/s/41348/ | datasoundsample | Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) |
| birds.wav | "Wings.wav" and "Wings2.wav" from the *Ninja Adventure* asset pack, https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack | Pixel-boy & AAA | CC0 1.0 (public domain) |

Changes: trimmed, shortened to about 1 second, converted to mono 22 kHz, loudness evened out;
the wing sounds are layered three times so they sound like several birds taking off.

The shutter, click and chime sounds are generated by the program itself (src/sound.cpp).
